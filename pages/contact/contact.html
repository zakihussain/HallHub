Frequently asked

How do I book a hall?

Start with Find a Hall, open a venue that feels right and use the booking panel to select your date, guests, catering and decoration.

Can I customise catering?

Yes. Choose from Silver, Gold and Royal packages. Your selected guest count updates the catering total live.

How can I change my booking?

For this prototype, send us a message with your booking ID and the HallHub team will help coordinate the update.

How does payment work?

The checkout is a safe simulation for this front-end prototype. It never charges a card or stores sensitive payment details.
